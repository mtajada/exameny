# Code of conduct

Exameny welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Treat people with respect and review the work, not the person.

## Expected conduct

- give specific, useful feedback;
- accept corrections without personal attacks;
- respect privacy and consent;
- credit other people's work;
- make space for questions from new contributors;
- keep educational examples free from stereotypes and humiliation.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate disruption, and publishing private communications are not accepted. Uploading material without the right to share it also breaches this code.

## Reporting

Use the private contact method listed on the lead maintainer's
[`@mtajada`](https://github.com/mtajada) profile with the subject
`Exameny conduct report`. If the report concerns that maintainer, ask a trusted
third party to receive it and state the conflict in the subject line.

Do not post a conduct report in a public issue. Include the relevant links, dates, and a short factual description. Project maintainers will limit access to people handling the report and will avoid sharing the reporter's identity without consent, except where safety or law requires it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
or ban an account. A maintainer named in a report must not decide that report.
The governance document describes how an independent reviewer takes over while
the project has only one maintainer.
