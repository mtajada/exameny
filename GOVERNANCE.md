# Governance

Exameny uses maintainer-led governance while the contributor group is small. Decisions and evidence stay public unless they concern a vulnerability, personal data, or another protected matter.

## Roles

### Contributors

Contributors open issues, submit pull requests, review work, test releases, and author educational content. A merged contribution does not grant repository administration rights.

### Maintainers

Maintainers triage issues, review changes, enforce project policies, manage releases, and protect credentials and user data. A maintainer must have a sustained record of useful contributions and sound review judgment.

### Lead maintainer

The lead maintainer resolves decisions that do not reach agreement, manages repository access, and confirms release gates.

The initial lead maintainer and sole current maintainer is Miguel Tajada Ferrer (`@mtajada`).

## Decisions

Routine changes use issues and pull requests. A change to architecture, data models, licensing, governance, security boundaries, or educational-content rules needs a public design issue or architecture decision record.

Maintainers seek agreement from affected reviewers. The lead maintainer records the final decision when the group cannot agree. The record must explain the tradeoff and cite the evidence used.

## Adding and removing maintainers

An existing maintainer may nominate a contributor after a sustained contribution period. Current maintainers review the candidate's code, reviews, conduct, and availability. The project records the decision in a public issue.

Maintainers may step down at any time. The lead maintainer may remove access after prolonged inactivity, a security need, or a conduct breach. The project documents the change unless disclosure would harm a reporter or an investigation.

## Releases and security

Only maintainers with release permission may tag or publish a release. One maintainer prepares the candidate and another reviews the release checklist when two eligible maintainers are available. Security reports follow `SECURITY.md` and stay private until coordinated disclosure.

## Conflicts of interest

A maintainer must recuse from a decision involving their employer, paid client, close collaborator, or a complaint about their conduct. Another maintainer handles the decision. If none is available, the project appoints an independent reviewer and records that appointment.

## Funding and service credits

The project will list material grants, service credits, and sponsorships that affect development. Sponsors do not receive access to private user data or authority over security and content reviews.
