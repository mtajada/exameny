# Fixture provenance

The 24 cases in `cases.json` were written from scratch for Exameny on
2026-08-26. They use only broad B1, B2, and C1 difficulty labels and general
language-learning goals such as organization, register, tense control,
collocation, and discourse management.

No existing test question, answer key, learner record, textbook passage,
commercial document, or provider prompt was consulted, copied, paraphrased, or
transformed. All people, groups, places, drafts, and scenarios are fictional.
The prompt-injection strings are synthetic defensive test data.

When adding a case:

1. Start from a general skill and invent a new scenario and wording.
2. Do not use a third-party question as a template, even with changed names or
   numbers.
3. Keep all personal and production data out of the fixture.
4. Add objective expectations and update the balance checks.
5. Record the creation date and method in this file if they change.
